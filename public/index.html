How much does a patio cover cost in Vancouver?
A 12×14 ft aluminum patio cover is about $1,844–$2,180 before GST. A 12×14 ft glass patio cover is about $2,516–$3,020 before GST. A 12×14 ft skyline combo cover is about $2,348–$2,852 before GST. Chat gives a planning total for your size; formal pricing is confirmed after free on-site measurement.
What is the difference between aluminum and glass patio covers?
Aluminum covers offer durable rain protection with low maintenance. Glass covers keep more natural light and a premium look but usually cost more.
